Suite Typology

Deluxe Suite

Ranging from 40 to 60 sqm, the Deluxe Suites form the foundation of the 24 Keys collection. Each one is designed as a calm and considered retreat, combining generous proportions, refined materiality and an intuitive sense of privacy.

Suite Typology

Signature Suite

With 95 to 100 sqm of space, the Signature Suites offer a more residential interpretation of the 24 Keys experience. Greater spatial generosity creates a natural distinction between rest, living and private moments, while maintaining the intimacy of the hotel's deliberately limited scale.

Suite Typology

Penthouse

Ranging from 250 to 300 sqm, the two Penthouses represent the most elevated and exclusive expression of 24 Keys. Conceived as private residences above Dubai, they offer exceptional scale, complete discretion and the highest level of personalisation within the hotel.

Warm minimalism, natural stone, marble and crafted details create a timeless identity designed to remain relevant.

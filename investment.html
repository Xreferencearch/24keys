Concept

Luxury, in Limited Edition.

An ultra-luxury boutique hotel in the UAE, conceived as a private destination where hospitality, design, gastronomy, culture and wellbeing come together for only 24 keys.

Behind 24 doors lies a private world shaped around the art of living well — a world of stillness, taste, sound, culture and restoration, hidden in plain sight.

Vision & Positioning

A boutique hotel, on the Cheval Blanc standard.

A boutique luxury hotel occupying the upper floors of a mixed-use tower, modelled on the Cheval Blanc standard, with 24 keys, a rooftop events space, a restaurant and bar aligned with the Le Coq formula, and a spa with a small gym.

The vision is deliberately compact — boutique is not a constraint but the core value proposition. Exclusivity at 24 rooms creates the perception of scarcity that underpins luxury pricing.

Financial Architecture

Beyond room revenue: a long-term ownership scheme.

Rather than relying solely on room revenue, the model layers a long-term room-ownership scheme — selling keys on 15-year contracts at approximately £3M per unit — to potentially recover the full capital outlay of £70–100M before operational profit is even counted.

The deeper strategic logic is that this first property is not the destination — it is the proof-of-concept that unlocks Maldives, New York, and multi-property scale.

Financial model based on a 20-year lease agreement; lease costs are included in operational assumptions.

Development Strategy

Conversion over new-build.

Rather than constructing an entirely new building, the preferred strategy is to acquire or lease an existing premium commercial property and convert it into an iconic luxury boutique hotel — faster to open, with lower development risk and an improved return on investment.

Risk & Delivery

The primary risk is concentration: everything hinges on a tight two-year delivery timeline, a credible financial model from the Financial Director, and a presentation compelling enough to attract investors before a single room is built.

Brand Pillars

Five ideas the brand is built on.

01

Boutique — A more personal scale of luxury.

Only 24 rooms: 16 Deluxe Suites, 6 Signature Suites and 2 Penthouses. Exclusivity is born from serving fewer guests with a far greater level of personalisation.

02

A Destination Within the Destination

Everything you came for, all under one roof. Not simply a place to sleep — a destination composed of gastronomy, bar, listening room, wine cellar, spa, art, events and rooftop.

03

Timeless design, made to outlast trends.

The aesthetic is minimalist, warm and enduring — an identity built on natural stone, marble and craftsmanship, designed to remain relevant for more than a decade.

04

A Cultural Point of View — A custodian of culture.

Art, design, gastronomy, sound, wine and carefully selected objects. The hotel can be read as a cultural house, not merely as accommodation.

05

Scale

This hotel is the first visible asset of a future international brand. The investor is not only investing in a property, but potentially in the origin of a hospitality platform.
